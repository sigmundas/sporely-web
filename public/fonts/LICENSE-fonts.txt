Self-hosted font files
======================

Sporely bundles these fonts directly so the Capacitor Android build's first
paint does not depend on fonts.googleapis.com. All three families are
released under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1, which allows
redistribution as part of any software so long as the OFL notice is retained
and the fonts themselves are not sold on their own.

fonts.gstatic.com and correspond to the following @font-face declarations
